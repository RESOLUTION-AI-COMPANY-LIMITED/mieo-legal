Delete your Mieo account

Mieo is a language-learning app published by Nguyen Cao Hoai Nam. This page explains how to delete your Mieo account and the data linked to it.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Mieo and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Delete it by email

If you cannot open the app, email contacts@sant1ago.dev from the address you signed up with. Put "Delete my Mieo account" in the subject. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name, sign-in identifiers, and your hashed password.
  • Your learning data: progress, XP, hearts, streak, and your setup choices.
  • Your ad-reward history that is linked to your account.

What is kept

  • Analytics collected by Google Firebase is not linked to your Mieo account. Google keeps it under the retention settings we set in Firebase.
  • Advertising data held by Google AdMob follows Google's privacy policy.
  • Nothing else is kept once your account is deleted, except where the law requires us to.
