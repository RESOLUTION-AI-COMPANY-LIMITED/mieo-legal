Mieo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Mieo ("the app") is a language-learning app published by Nguyen Cao Hoai Nam ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and your choices. Mieo is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Data we collect

Account data. When you sign up with email or Google, we collect your email address, your display name, and a Google account identifier if you use Google sign-in. If you sign up with email, we store your password in hashed form only.

Learning data. Your lesson progress, XP, hearts, streak, and the language choices you make during setup. We also store your device time zone so that daily hearts and streaks reset at the right time.

Device and usage data. Through Firebase Analytics we collect app interactions (screens viewed, lessons started and finished), device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Firebase Remote Config receives a pseudonymous installation identifier to deliver app settings.

Crash and performance data. Through Firebase Crashlytics we collect crash reports (stack traces, the app's state at the time of the crash, and device information such as model and operating system version) and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Through Firebase Performance Monitoring we collect app performance data such as app start time, network request latency, CPU and memory use, and your IP address to group results by country. We use this data to find and fix problems.

Advertising data. Mieo shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and some US states, we ask for your consent before ads are personalised.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device. These are generated locally and do not send data to us.

How we use data

  • To create and secure your account, and to save your progress.
  • To send account emails (email verification and password reset), through Amazon SES.
  • To understand how the app is used and to fix problems.
  • To show and measure ads.

We do not sell your personal data.

These providers process data under their own privacy terms. Google: policies.google.com/privacy. AWS: aws.amazon.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • Ad personalisation: change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app settings or in Android settings.
  • Delete your account: see how to delete your account.
  • Access, correction, or deletion requests: email contacts@sant1ago.dev.

Data retention

We keep account and learning data while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account. Analytics data is retained by Google according to the retention settings we configure in Firebase.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your data, and to object to processing. Contact us at contacts@sant1ago.dev.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
